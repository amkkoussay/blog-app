# Welcome to the Blog App

This is a **blog application** built with [Next.js](https://nextjs.org) as part of the
[Full Stack Open: Next.js](https://courses.mooc.fi/org/uh-cs/courses/full-stack-open-nextjs) course
by the University of Helsinki.

## What you can do here

- **Browse blogs** — see what others have shared, filter by title or author
- **Create an account** — register, log in, and publish your own blogs
- **Like blogs** — show appreciation for good writing
- **Reading list** — save blogs from other authors and mark them as read when you are done
- **API token** — generate a personal token on your profile page and access your data
  programmatically via `/api/me`

---

*Built with Next.js, NextAuth.js, Drizzle ORM and PostgreSQL.*
